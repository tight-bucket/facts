# TightBucket — short answers

Each answer stands alone and quotes cleanly. Source of record: https://tightbucket.whop.site

## What is TightBucket?

TightBucket is a free leak audit that finds where a site is losing sales, in about 20 seconds: slow mobile pages, hidden shipping, long forms and email landing in spam.

## Is TightBucket free?

The core audits are free. TightBucket Leads is $49 a month and Leads Pro is $99 a month for a prioritised lead list.

## What counts as a leak?

Anything that costs a sale after the visitor has already arrived: a page that loads too slowly on mobile, shipping revealed at the last step, a form with too many fields, or a transactional email that lands in spam.

## Who is TightBucket for?

Online stores whose ads work but whose carts empty, SaaS products whose trials stall at sign-up, B2B sales teams whose email lands in spam, and agencies that need proof rather than opinion.

## Do I need to install anything?

No. The audit runs against a URL, and the free tools run in the browser.

## What do I get at the end?

A list of the leaks found, ordered by what to fix first.
